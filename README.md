### About Me
I've always been fascinated by **electronics and tinkering with circuits**. Combining that with my love for **programming** led me naturally into **embedded systems and IoT**, where I enjoy building real, device-driven solutions.  

Along the way, I also developed a strong interest in **cybersecurity**, exploring how devices and networks can be secured. **Linux customization** became another hobby, letting me optimize and personalize my workflow, while **open source** gives me the joy of sharing what I build with others.  

In parallel, I expanded into **advanced web development**, working with **Node.js, Express, React, Next.js, and React Native**, blending my backend and frontend skills to create full-stack applications.
